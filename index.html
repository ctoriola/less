Who I am

I make records from the tension between the sacred and the reckless. Every song is a confession—lit carefully, then left to burn.

Lord, Saint, Sinner is the solo project of one restless voice, shaped between Lagos and London. No committee, no corporate distance: just the work, the word and the room it creates around you.
